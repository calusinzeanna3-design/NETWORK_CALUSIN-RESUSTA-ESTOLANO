Introduction

NEW ENHANCED TEAM WITH ORIENTED KNOWLEDGE

The New Enhanced Team With Oriented Knowledge (NETWORK) is a student-led organization committed to empowering future professionals through seminars, workshops, and collaborative activities focused on innovation and leadership.

NETWORK builds a strong academic community by promoting teamwork, enhancing technical skills, and fostering growth among BIT students.

Through its initiatives, the organization prepares members to adapt and thrive in a fast-changing technological environment.

What we do

Growing leaders inside BIT

NETWORK is more than an organization – it’s a community of learners, leaders, and innovators working together to make BIT truly future-ready. Below are our core pillars and how we deliver value to students.

Leadership & Growth

We develop leadership through hands-on projects, student-led events, and mentorship programs that allow members to take real responsibilities — from planning to execution and reflection.

  • Project leadership opportunities
  • Workshops on public speaking & team ops
  • Mentorship & peer coaching

Technology & Skills

Practical tech sessions, code labs, and tool-based workshops (web, mobile, databases, cloud) that turn theory into usable skills for internships and projects.

  • Hands-on workshops & hackathons
  • Guided projects with deliverables
  • Guest speakers from industry

Community & Service

We build a supportive campus culture through volunteer activities, outreach programs, and collaborations that let students apply skills for social good.

  • Community outreach & service projects
  • Inter-organization collaborations
  • Volunteer-driven initiatives

Frequently Asked Questions

What is NETWORK?

NETWORK stands for New Enhanced Team With Oriented Knowledge, a student-led BIT organization focused on leadership, technology, and community service.

How do I join NETWORK?

Visit the Join Us page and fill out the membership form. Selected applicants may be invited to an orientation or short interview.

What are the membership requirements?

Open to current BIT students. We expect active participation in at least one project or committee and attendance at core meetings. A positive attitude and willingness to learn are more important than prior experience.

How can I propose an event or project?

Send a short proposal to the officers via the contact email or present it during the Projects meeting. Include objectives, estimated resources, and a proposed timeline.

When and where are NETWORK activities held?

Activities are posted on the News page and our social channels. Events happen on campus or online—check each event's details for date, time, and venue.

Who can I contact for sponsorship or partnership?

Email our partnerships officer at support@bitcourse.com (subject: Sponsorship). Include a brief description of your organization, proposed partnership, and benefit to students.

Is there a code of conduct?

Yes — all members must follow our code of conduct: respect others, act professionally, be inclusive, and avoid harassment. Violations may lead to suspension or removal from the organization.

How do I report issues (e.g., content problems, abuse, technical issues)?

Report to support@bitcourse.com or contact an officer directly. For sensitive issues, request a confidential meeting with the executive committee.

Where can I find resources to learn new skills?

NETWORK maintains a resources list shared to members. We also organize workshops and peer mentoring for web dev, data, hardware, and soft skills.

How can I become an officer?

Officer positions are elected/appointed based on the constitution/bylaws. Watch for announcements (usually at semester start) and prepare a short platform or show recent contributions.
